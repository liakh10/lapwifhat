Everything about the laptop and the hat Mostly the hat

01 · OVERVIEWWhat is Laptop Wif Hat

A green laptop with a face on the screen and a knitted pink hat on its lid. It sits on a porch railing in Delaware and looks at the street. That is the whole thing, the same way a dog with a hat was the whole thing.

Everything looks like a 2003 console game because the laptop stopped updating that year and the hat never needed to.

HERO
One green low poly laptop, two dot eyes, flat mouth, pink beanie
PLACE
A front porch at golden hour, a rocking chair, a mailbox, a lawn
THE FORMAT
Wif hat. The hat is the personality, the laptop is the body
CHAIN
Robinhood Chain, token through the Pons launchpad
